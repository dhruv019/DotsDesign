SHILP RESIDENCE

A modern contemporary interior design project blending elegance and functionality to create a cozy space.

About the project

The project was a recently completed interior design endeavor, undertaken with the goal of crafting a personalized and functional space that reflected the client's preferences and lifestyle.
The brief from the client revolved around creating a contemporary yet timeless ambiance, with a focus on comfort, elegance, and a seamless blend of aesthetics and functionality.
Project Residential renovation and interiors
Area 1800 sq.ft
Location Bhayli, Vadodara
GALLERY
They desired a space that would embody sophistication while maintaining a cozy and inviting atmosphere for the family.
The design style employed in this project can be best described as modern contemporary with subtle influences of minimalism.
Clean lines, uncluttered spaces, and a harmonious balance of textures and materials define the overall aesthetic.
The inclusion of neutral palettes, sleek furniture, and carefully chosen décor accents ensures a contemporary look, while warm lighting and soft furnishings introduce a sense of coziness.
