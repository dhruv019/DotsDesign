ABOUT THE PROJECT

This sophisticated 5,000 square foot residence exemplifies contemporary luxury through its thoughtfully curated neutral palette and architectural lighting. The primary spaces include an elegant master bedroom with artistic wall treatments, a formal dining area anchored by a crystal chandelier, and a well-appointed living room featuring refined furnishings.
Throughout the home, exceptional attention to detail is evident in the high-end finishes, custom millwork, and premium materials, from warm wood paneling to metallic accents and textural fabrics.
